# Standard Syntax

## Clause

Default:
**Subject – finite verb – object – other material.**

Example:
Ik ser en bok.

## Questions

Finite verb precedes subject:
Ser du boken?

Question word first:
Var bor du?

## Negation

Main:
Ik ser boken inte.

Subordinate:
... att ik inte ser boken.

## Subordination

Conjunction + subject + sentence adverb + finite verb.

Ik wet att du inte kommer.

## Relative clauses

som introduces relative clauses:
en bok som ik läser

## Topicalization

A constituent may move to the first position while the finite verb stays in second position:
I dag ser ik boken.

## Coordination

och, eller, men, så.

## Information structure

Contrastive fronting is allowed:
Den boken ser ik inte.

Historical corpus word order is never rewritten merely to satisfy the standard.
