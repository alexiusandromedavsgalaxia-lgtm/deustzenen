## Confirmed historical rule

ß → s
ßß → b

Example:
saßßen → seben

## Prosody

Default lexical stress is normally initial/root stress.
Compounds carry primary stress on the first member.
Swedish-like pitch accents are permitted as a stylistic pronunciation feature but are not phonemic in v0.1.

## Phonotactics

Preferred syllable:
(C)(C)(C)V(V)(C)(C)

Common initial clusters:
sp, st, sk, sl, sm, sn, sw, sj, tj, br, dr, fr, gr, kr, pl, tr

Word-final clusters are permitted but restricted.

## Sandhi

In rapid speech:
- n may assimilate in place to a following consonant;
- s may voice before voiced obstruents;
- unstressed vowels may reduce.

These are CONSTRUCTED phonetic tendencies.
