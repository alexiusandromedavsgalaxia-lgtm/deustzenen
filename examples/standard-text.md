# Deustzenen Standard Text

## Neutral prose

Ik bor in en liten stad. Där har ik en bok och en språkbok. Var morgon läser ik i boken, och om kvällen skriver ik nya ord. Ik weta att språk förändras, men ik vill behålla de gamla formerna.

## Translation

I live in a small city. There I have a book and a language book. Every morning I read in the book, and in the evening I write new words. I know that languages change, but I want to preserve the old forms.

## Note

This text is an intentionally constructed standard example. It is not being presented as a translation recovered from the historical corpus.
