# Grapheme → Sound Correspondences

Status: mixed: CONFIRMED historical rules plus CONSTRUCTED standard values.

## Confirmed

ß → s
ßß → b

Corpus example:
saßßen → seben

## Standard correspondences

a → /a/
e → /e ~ ɛ/
i → /i/
o → /o ~ ɔ/
u → /ʉ ~ u/
y → /y/
ä → /ɛ ~ æ/
ö → /ø ~ œ/
å → /ɔ ~ o/
j → /j/
sj → /ɧ ~ ʃ/
sk → /sk/ or /ɧ/ before front vowels
ch → /x ~ ç/

## Important principle

These correspondences describe the modern standard. They must not be used to erase or rewrite historical corpus spellings.
