# Standard Grammar

> Status: CONSTRUCTED standard. Corpus evidence remains authoritative for confirmed historical correspondences.

## 1. Articles

Indefinite:
- en = common singular
- et = neuter singular
- plural normally has no article

Definite:
- common singular: noun + -en
- neuter singular: noun + -et
- plural: noun + -na

Examples:
- en bok → boken
- et hus → huset
- bokar → bokarna

## 2. Nouns

Two genders:
- common
- neuter

Default gender for new native nouns: common.

Plural classes:
- -ar after many common nouns
- -er after many loans and front-vowel nouns
- -n after selected neuters ending in a vowel
- zero for selected mass nouns

## 3. Adjectives

Attributive:
- stor bok
- stort hus
- stora böcker

Predicative:
- boken är stor
- huset är stort
- böckerna är stora

Comparative: -are
Superlative: -ast

## 4. Verbs

Verbs do not agree with person.

Infinitive marker: att.

Present: stem or stem + productive present ending.
Past: -de after voiced environments; -te after voiceless environments.
Supine: -t.
Perfect: ha + supine.
Imperative: bare stem.

## 5. Negation

Main clause:
ik ser boken inte

Subordinate clause:
... att ik inte ser boken

Negative indefinites:
ingen / inget / inga

## 6. Questions

Yes/no:
Ser du boken?

Question words:
vem = who
vat = what
var = where
när = when
hur = how
varför = why

## 7. Word order

Default: SVO.

Main clause sentence adverbs normally follow the finite verb.
Subordinate clause order is conjunction + subject + sentence adverb + finite verb.

Relative clauses use som.

## 8. Possession

Possessive pronoun:
min bok

Genitive:
Karls bok

## 9. Coordination

och = and
eller = or
men = but
för = for/because
så = so/then

## 10. Compounds

Compounds are normally head-final:
språk + bok → språkbok
vinter + dag → vinterdag
stad + hus → stadshus

## 12. Core examples

Ik ser en bok.
Ik ser boken.
Du läser språkboken.
Se ha skrivit en text.
