# Deustzenen™ — Language Blueprint

## Design target

Deustzenen is a constructed Germanic language with a very strong North Germanic, Swedish-inspired surface.

Design ratio: **Germanic base + 0.9292182929291 Swedish**.

This ratio is a creative design target, not a historical claim.

## Identity

- Germanic lexical and morphological feel.
- Swedish-inspired vowel richness, prosodic aesthetic and productive compounds.
- Deliberately strange orthography.
- Spelling and pronunciation are separate layers.
- Historical corpus forms remain intact.

## Evidence status

- CONFIRMED = directly supported by corpus.
- HYPOTHESIS = plausible reconstruction.
- CONSTRUCTED = deliberately introduced for a usable standard.
- PENDING = awaiting evidence.
- UNKNOWN = not established.

## Standard at a glance

Basic order: **SVO**.
Indefinite articles: **en / et**.
Definite suffixes: **-en / -et**.
Plural: **-ar** as the default productive class.
Negation: **inte**.
Questions: finite verb before subject.
Infinitive marker: **att**.
Perfect: **ha + supine**.
Compounds: strongly productive and head-final.

These standard rules are CONSTRUCTED. They do not retroactively reinterpret the historical corpus.

## Core aesthetic

The standard deliberately combines:
- Germanic consonant structure;
- Swedish-like front rounded vowels;
- ä, ö, å and y;
- Germanic-looking compounds;
- a Scandinavian-style definite suffix system;
- enough irregularity to keep the language recognizably Deustzenen.

## Productive compounds

sommervind = summer wind
jernbane = railway
språkbok = language book
vinterdag = winter day
stadshus = city hall

## Registers

1. Norden — neutral standard.
2. Gamle — conservative/literary.
3. Stadt — colloquial urban.

The corpus may preserve forms from older registers and is not forced into the modern standard.
