# Standard Orthography

## Alphabet

a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x y z

Special graphemes:
ä ö ü å ß ş ḍ ð þ

## Principles

1. Orthography is deliberately conservative and Germanic-looking.
2. Pronunciation is not mechanically recoverable from spelling.
3. ß is retained in historical and stylistic writing.
4. ş marks /ʃ/ in transparent modern spelling.
5. j marks /j/.
6. w is retained in native-looking Germanic vocabulary.
7. Macrons are optional in pedagogical phonemic transcription.

## Historical spelling

- historical ß may be preserved;
- normalized spelling may use s where pronunciation is /s/;
- ßß remains the special historical sequence corresponding to /b/ in the confirmed corpus.

## Capitalization

Modern standard Deustzenen normally uses lower-case nouns. Literary Germanic style may capitalize nouns.
